# Workout Plan

All sets taken to failure at ~9 reps, progressing with weight over time (progressive overload for hypertrophy).

## Upper Body (2x/week)

- 2x incline bench press
- 2x chest flyes
- 2x pull downs
- 2x wide grip rows
- 2x bicep curls
- 2x shoulder press
- 2x tricep rope pull down

## Lower Body (1x/week)

- 3x barbell back squat
- 3x leg press
- 3x leg extensions
- 3x stiff-legged deadlift
- 3x lying leg curls
- 3x walking lunges
- 3x standing calf raises

## Core (2–3x/week, end of any session)

**Lower abs**

- 3x hanging leg raises

**Upper abs**

- 3x cable crunches

**Obliques**

- 3x cable woodchoppers

**Deep core**

- 3x weighted plank or ab wheel rollouts
